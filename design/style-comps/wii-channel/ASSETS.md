# Assets

## Font

**M PLUS Rounded 1c**, by Coji Morishita and the M+ Fonts Project. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- `fonts/MPLUSRounded1c-Medium-latin.woff2` (weight 500) and `fonts/MPLUSRounded1c-ExtraBold-latin.woff2` (weight 800): the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts (`https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/mplusrounded1c/v22/…`), from the family page `https://fonts.google.com/specimen/M+PLUS+Rounded+1c`.
- Upstream source: `https://github.com/coz-m/MPLUS_FONTS`; Google Fonts copy: `https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mplusrounded1c`.
- Licence text: `fonts/OFL.txt`. It came from the sibling M+ family directory in google/fonts (`ofl/mplus1p/OFL.txt`), because the Rounded 1c directory carries no separate file. The terms are the same OFL 1.1 and the same M+ authors.

## Everything else

No other downloaded assets. The slabs, bevels, rings, shadows, halos, wrist pucks, icons and wall are all procedural: generated meshes, small GLSL shaders and 2D canvas drawing at runtime. Playlist covers come from `_shared/content.mjs` through `coverUrl()`, as the brief requires.
