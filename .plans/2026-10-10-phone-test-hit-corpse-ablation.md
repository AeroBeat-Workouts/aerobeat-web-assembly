# Phone Test-mode Flow hit and corpse ablation

**Date:** 2026-10-10. **Status:** approved by Derrick; queued until the separate CV/visual/shadow diagnostic is landed and handed off. **Owner:** `aerobeat-web-assembly` `main`. **Tracking:** umbrella `aerobeat-web-assembly-e218`; implementation `t2i8`, QA `tiog`, audit `ev1y` (all created, unclaimed until this second slice starts). **Goal round cap:** Maximum safe integer.

## Decision and observable problem

Derrick tested Cookie's latest AeroBeat playtest on his phone: in **Test mode without CV**, hitting Flow beats with the sword and spawning corpses dropped the scene below 30 FPS. This shows the CV submission timer is not the only performance issue. He approved a **separate phone hit-versus-no-hit diagnostic after** `.plans/2026-10-10-phone-visual-tracking-shadow-ablation.md` lands. Keep that first page's four modes, production CV scheduling/model, scored Play, immutable releases and the existing GUI route unchanged. Use latest published Git commits, record them in every build and report, and never claim Cookie served-build or phone FPS parity from a host fixture.

Read-only research `e218` traced Flow hit to real/synthetic judgement (`aerobeat-web-gameplay/src/session-coordinator.js`), target feedback (`src/session-render-projection.js`), persistent aftermath projection (`src/gameplay-frame-effects.js`) and PlayCanvas corpse materials/pools (`aerobeat-web-renderer/src/renderer-facade.js`). A hit yields feedback and two clipped corpse halves. The renderer pools corpse entities but may first compile material/shader variants; per-frame event/judgement scans may also cost CPU. **Corpse entities do not cast shadows** (`renderer-facade.js:598`). Native shadows might lower the baseline but are not established as the cause of the hit-time hitch. The current phone workload has no judgements/aftermath, so its shadow comparison cannot reproduce this report.
