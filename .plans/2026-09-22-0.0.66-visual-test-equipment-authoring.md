## Direct playtest intake

Derrick confirmed equipment is visible in Test and requested:

1. Equipment must remain visible when Visual Test transport is paused.

## Declared scope and invariants

- Version target: 0.0.66 after diagnosis, coder/QA/audit, full browser gates, and target-hardware gate.

- Raw 0.0.65 and all earlier accepted releases remain byte-immutable and served until successor GO.

- New Test controls are private authoring state only: no public snapshots, events, iframe payloads, storage, persistence, scoring, judgement records, input/CV mutation, or camera acquisition.
- Defaults preserve current behavior: auto feedback on; mouse-hand off; Test equipment visibility off on a fresh connection/session.
- Paused Test may render equipment and accept authoring pointer/config changes without advancing its timeline or producing gameplay truth. Normal Play session/tracking/menu suppression remains fail-closed.
- Pointer coordinates are finite and clamped to the renderer content box; leaving/canceling/disconnecting/resetting disables active pointer evidence safely.
- Existing free-fly camera controls remain usable when mouse-hand control is off; any conflict while a hand is selected must be explicit and deterministic.
