**Observed:** The supplied screenshot shows the cyan glass ending in a straight edge across the lower face, with camera image below and an opaque bottom control strip; no saber or glove is visible, so equipment shadow visibility cannot be concluded directly. Derrick reports the mobile GUI stays fully opaque regardless of his touch attempts, unlike desktop. **Expected:** The visual track and invisible receiver occupy the near region behind/under the bottom UI in portrait; real equipment shadows may become visible when posed there. With menu closed, gameplay active and no touch in progress, bottom controls dim; touching them makes them legible immediately; after touch end/cancel they dim again rather than sticking opaque. Desktop and landscape retain truthful gameplay framing and control accessibility.

**Execution path:** Assembly `rendererFrame()` forwards configured `trackExtensionWorldUnits` (default 2) to renderer; renderer `buildGameplaySceneModel/addTrack` builds three unscaled 24-WU glass segments ending at that value, and syncs shadow receiver to those tracks. `applyProductionCameraPose()` computes aspect-responsive vertical FOV for 4×3 play grid. Narrow aspect widens vertical FOV while the near track end remains at Z≈2; camera near region can enter view. For UI, assembly `src/index.js` installs document pointermove/touchmove; only `handleBottomUiPointer` updates transport CSS variable `--aero-bottom-ui-opacity` from 1 to .3; web-ui transport defaults to opacity 1 and has unconditional `:hover` → 1. Mobile touch has no reliable pointermove outside the bottom bar and may retain hover after a tap; no release/end listener resets it. CSS background remains ~.9 at opacity 1.

**Proven root causes:** In real tailnet HTTPS Chromium mobile emulation at 393×852, with renderer assets ready and default extension 2, camera FOV is 100.029° and the track near edge projects at Y640.36/852, leaving 212 CSS px with no glass; the receiver AABB follows that GLB end. Control 852×393 uses FOV48° and near edge Y461.3/393, safely offscreen. The screenshot shown to us is 497×411 pixels, whose aspect would not explain the edge as a *full canonical canvas*: it is likely an image crop or different layout, so this 393×852 full-screen reproduction is the decisive geometry evidence. Assembly transport is hidden with menu open but its CSS variable and computed opacity are 1; after setting menu closed and making bar visible, it remains 1 until a synthetic far `pointermove` sets variable .3. A 400ms transition later computed opacity is .3; touch-only idle does not generate that far move. No touch-end reset exists. Unconditional mobile hover may be sticky, but in our emulated tap case `:hover` became true while computed opacity remained .3, so hover alone was not the observed necessary cause. Distinguish initial inactivity and touch release with product-owned lifecycle test before final fix.

**Alternatives:** Mobile address-bar/safe-area viewport height or CSS parent sizing could alter aspect and visual boundary (measure rendered host and device viewport). `trackExtensionWorldUnits` might be customized rather than default (inspect live setup). Camera feed is independently behind track, so its visibility below the straight edge does not prove a WebGL draw failure.
