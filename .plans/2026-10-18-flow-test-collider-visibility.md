## Debugging report — before product edits

### Exact observed failure

Derrick: “The collider is not visible in this build in test mode, collider visible enabled and collider size set to 1 and forward depth set to 1.” He clarified that **the saber itself is visible** (Equipment preview on). On frozen signed 0.0.131 with native Flow Show=on, Scale1, Depth+1, Depth−0, active Test and Equipment preview on, the right green GLB saber is visible but a separate collider is not perceptible. Direct signed 844×390 GPU capture `.tmp/flow-test-131-before.png` shows the narrow bright blade. Same-frame PlayCanvas on/off readback changes only **151 pixels** (`111` with RGB delta≥35) out of 329,160, mostly on a 7-pixel-high blade strip. That is not a readable diagnostic overlay. No page error occurs.

### Expected behavior

The Show setting promises a visible representation of the *same* scored Flow capsule geometry, even at Scale1 and in actual Test mode. Its user-facing outline must remain distinguishable without widening scored contact, enlarging note targets, hiding the saber, or breaking Play/Boxing. Lost wrist and Show off must still remove it.

### Execution path

Test starts with `src/index.js:startSession('visual_test')` and Equipment preview is enabled separately; `renderGameplay()` resolves two Flow equipment poses. At line2297 it attaches exactly those visible poses as `frame.equipmentColliderPoses` to the renderer frame. `gameplay-scene-model.js:186–207` sees Show=true and constructs per-hand capsules at radius `.024 × pose.scale × Scale` with `.16` opacity; right scale2 gives radius `.048` and an active 1.5-WU centerline. `renderer-facade.js:686–702` creates and enables GPU capsule meshes, and `applyColliderOverlayAppearance` at lines737–755 assigns the same hand color, depth testing on and a dedicated transparent layer. The signed Test trace confirmed session purpose visual_test playing, run-locked Show=true/Scale1/+1/−0, two Flow GLB poses, two correctly placed scene capsules, two enabled GPU entities with opacity .16 and depthTest=true, and no lastError. Thus settings, Test pose generation and mesh staging **do work**; visual compositing/perceptibility fails.

### Most likely root cause — causally confirmed in the frozen build

`renderer-facade.js:530` inserts the collider layer **before PlayCanvas World transparent**, where the GLB saber is rendered; `applyColliderOverlayAppearance` also depth-tests translucent same-hue `.16` material against the opaque/coincident solid blade. At Scale1 the collider radius exactly coincides with the solid blade, so art painted after it (plus coplanar depth) erases most of the fill. In a *temporary runtime-only* Test diagnostic with identical signed bytes/poses/frame and no source modification, the collider layer index was `7` versus World transparent `10`. Compare true/false on the same framebuffer: baseline 151 changed pixels. Moving the collider layer to *after* World transparent gave 1,600 changed pixels; additionally switching its material depthTest off gave 2,297; then using opacity `.6` gave 2,676 changed pixels (2,237 with RGB delta≥35). `.tmp/flow-test-131-layer-after-highopacity.png` records the temporary upper-bound look. These within-frame interventions distinguish this compositing cause from absent pose/mesh and show a visible correction is possible without changing scored geometry. The test did not yet establish the best final outline design or its phone perceptibility.

### Alternatives ranked

1. **Dominant: collider rendered under same-width GLB with weak same-hue alpha/depth** — directly supported by signed GPU on/off and temporary layer/depth causal interventions.
2. **Test preview disabled** — initially plausible from the default-off Test toggle; Derrick explicitly reports visible saber, and the reproduced Test has preview enabled with GLB asset mode, so not his case.
3. **Run-locked Show/Scale/Depth setting silently ignored** — excluded by activeSessionSetup and per-hand scene/GPU geometry at `.048` radius.
4. **Tracking loss** — Test preview poses were both valid; a separate phone tracking issue remains untested, but cannot explain this signed Test capture.
5. **Public stale build/cache** — both public HTTPS relays previously returned exact candidate bytes and signed direct Test reproduces, so not required to explain failure.

### Why previous fix failed

0.0.131 correctly changed scoring/Show geometry from a wide radius `.18 × pose.scale` to the solid blade radius `.024 × pose.scale`. Previous acceptance checked scored Play hits, GPU radius/mesh and an aligned (partly clipped) screenshot, **not the on/off pixel contrast of the outline in actual Test**. The new Test oracle runs DPR1 in its normal browser gate; `AEROBEAT_FLOW_TEST_DPR=3` is an opt-in phone-density visual check for the thin line, not a substitute for physical handset judgement. The earlier broad width had a visible exterior; shrinking to exact art width made existing behind-art translucent rendering nearly disappear. Enlarging the *scored* collider back would violate Derrick's approved Scale1 meaning and is not an acceptable fix.

Recompose the Show capsule on top of the opaque and transparent saber art and draw an unmistakable **thin high-contrast debug edge** at the true radius/endpoints; keep the underlying scored geometry and Show gating unchanged. A mere layer reorder or depth-off at the existing `.16` same-hue fill may still look like the blade rather than a distinguishable collider. Avoid enlarging the scored capsule or changing authored GLB art. Derrick selected the true-size contrasting outline, explicitly rejecting a mere stronger translucent tint.
