## GATE 1-R1 — Saber design v2 (Derrick, 2026-09-19, after reviewing the r1 model in Blender)

1. **Saber = TWO joined cylinders** (classic lightsaber silhouette), NOT a single tapered prism:
   - **Hilt** — a SHORT cylinder. Its ORIGIN attaches to the athlete's wrist marker (the grip point, where the athlete holds the saber). Dark/structural (gunmetal), NOT per-hand tinted.
   - **Blade** — a LONGER cylinder, joined to the hilt's far end, extending further in the same direction. This is the per-hand TINTABLE emissive section (carries the song-palette color).
   - **Rounded tip** on the blade (hemispherical cap, blade radius).
   - The two are joined and collinear along the local blade axis (local +Y; the engine rotates to the motion direction, unchanged from r1).
2. **In-engine glow around the BLADE section only** (this is what makes it a lightsaber). The glow is an additive layer the renderer stages around the blade (not baked into the GLB); glow color == blade tint color. The hilt has NO glow.
3. **What-you-see-is-what-hits preserved:** total visible length (hilt + blade + tip) == the 0.75 WU detection capsule, hilt base at the wrist (origin). The hilt occupies the near end of the capsule; the blade the far end. (A sub-pixel tip-rounding overshoot is acceptable; the lane reports exact figures.)
4. **Dimming must stay visible (r1 defect rule):** the additive glow cannot dim via opacity (PlayCanvas additive ignores source alpha) — dim it by SCALING its emissive/color × 0.45. The normal-blend blade dims via opacity (proven in r1). Both must produce a measurable dimmed-vs-undimmed pixel delta (Aero + Camera).
5. **Suggested starting dims (lane may tune, must report):** hilt ≈ 0.18 WU long × r 0.030; blade ≈ 0.55 WU long × r 0.024 (slightly thinner than the hilt); tip hemisphere r = blade radius. Hilt:blade ≈ 1:3.
6. **Review artifact format (applies to saber AND glove, Derrick's explicit ask):** generate ANGLEd, PULLED-BACK 3D renders of the model (headless Blender F12 with a camera, a few angles × both color pairs × dimmed/undimmed) as the PRIMARY visual review images — like Derrick's own Blender screenshot — NOT just flat in-game pixel crops. In-game sweep rows remain the pixel-truth + dimming-proof.
