## Exact Observed Failure

Derrick reports:

1. Boxing Grid obstacles render as single-cell, non-continuous geometry and do not use Flow's approved wall asset.
2. Boxing Lanes obstacles are absent or unobservable in gameplay.

Exact `3c9d` reproduction proves the event reaches both Boxing modes as `weave_right` with `blockedCells:[1]`, but has no geometry, end, or interval:

- Lanes renders one `any-note/circle-v1` at right lane X `1.35`, Y `1.1`, plus a primitive shadow.
- Grid renders one `any-note/circle-v1` at cell `1`, a primitive static blocked-cell overlay, and shadow.
- Neither mode instantiates `wall/red-glass-v1`.

Therefore Lanes data is not absent; its circle fallback is not physically identifiable as an obstacle. The separate vertical-normalization defect also limits Grid occupancy to one cell.

## Most Likely Root Cause

Compound contract/scope failure:

1. Boxing authoring intentionally discards normalized obstacle geometry and end after feasibility calculations.
2. Renderer explicitly limits continuous wall assets to Flow and routes all Boxing obstacles through generic circles.
3. The upstream v2 vertical-normalization bug contaminates Boxing blocked/safe cells, feasibility, Grid footprint, reach paths, and guard relocation with `[1]` instead of `[1,5,9]`.
4. Tests positively assert the current Boxing circle behavior and require wall assets only in Flow.
