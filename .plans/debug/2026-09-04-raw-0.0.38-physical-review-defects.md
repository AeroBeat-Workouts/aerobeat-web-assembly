# Raw 0.0.38 physical-review defects

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Bead:** `aerobeat-web-assembly-0wn`  
**Disposition:** Diagnosis in progress; no source/runtime/release correction is authorized by this record alone.  
**Immutable evidence:** Raw `0.0.38` remains rejected, byte-immutable evidence.

## Exact Observed Failure

Derrick physically tested raw `0.0.38` with live camera gameplay and observed:

1. Nose and wrist landmark spheres have the same clear/low-visibility appearance previously seen on directional notes.
2. In BeatSaver map `3c9d`, `Dance Dance Revolution [DDRMIX]`, obstacles near 37 seconds look like a full column in BeatSaver ArcViewer but appear as one square cell in AeroBeat.
3. The green/yellow/red timing sections look like a separate broad overlay instead of tinted lane tiles.
4. A missed note disappears at the athlete instead of continuing past in gray.
5. `Miss` is too small/static; it should be larger and shake left-right-back-and-forth briefly.
6. `Great` is too small/static; it should be larger and bounce before disappearing.
7. Beats and obstacles lack a directly-below shadow/depth cue.

Derrick explicitly confirmed that directional-note white outlines and the white right-timing flash look good. “More timing cues” is a future requirement without a current concrete contract; this diagnosis does not invent them.

The marker symptom is directly observed. Alpha transparency is not: exact asset/runtime evidence contradicts alpha as the cause.

## Expected Behavior

- Nose and wrist markers remain visibly solid and identifiable over live video, bright/dark regions, and all owned environments while retaining truthful landmark positions and depth.
- Imported obstacles retain a documented, source-faithful spatial interpretation. Visual occupancy and gameplay collision occupancy must not silently diverge.
- Timing intervals retain authoritative timing-window boundaries but are presented as lane-aligned tiles whose count/partial extent derives deterministically from the interval data.
- Hit scoring remains unchanged, with the confirmed white timing flash and bounded hit removal.
- Missed notes retain their identity, travel monotonically past the athlete, gray only their tintable role color, and disappear at a bounded deterministic endpoint.
- World feedback remains event-bound and deterministic: larger `Miss` with horizontal shake; larger `Great` with bounce.
- Shadows are renderer-only depth cues tied to visible beat/obstacle geometry. They never enter scoring, collision, judgement, persistence, telemetry, or authored content.

## Most Likely Root Cause

### Marker visibility

The leading cause is a visual-contract/test gap, not alpha transparency:

- The marker GLB is exactly one `0.18 × 0.18 × 0.18` UV sphere with one `mat/marker` material.
- Base alpha is `1`; glTF’s omitted `alphaMode` defaults to opaque.
- Runtime passes alpha `1`, retains opaque/depth-writing source behavior, enables depth test, and force-tints the only material.
- Unlike the corrected arrow, the marker has no preserved white/dark structural separator or camera-independent silhouette treatment.
- Assembly requests an apparent CSS size, but renderer uses fixed world scale.
- Existing tests prove marker presence/position/basic depth with a very low pixel threshold; they do not prove minimum area, alpha invariance, edge contrast, or live-camera/environment visibility.

The result is a small uniformly colored object that can lose edge and volume contrast and appear clear even though its geometry is opaque.

### Obstacle discrepancy

AeroBeat intentionally collapses legacy Beat Saber obstacle geometry into canonical cell occupancy, but the conversion is lossy and undocumented at the user-visible boundary. Specifically, legacy crouch-wall `y=2,h=3` continuous geometry is reduced to a single top-row cell. The renderer receives no source Y/height metadata and can only draw one square wall per canonical cell. ArcViewer retains the continuous volume, so the two products cannot match visually.

This diagnosis does not yet choose between:

- changing canonical collision cells,
- retaining collision cells but carrying separate source-faithful visual geometry, or
- defining another explicit AeroBeat adaptation.

That choice affects gameplay semantics and requires Derrick’s decision after the factual difference is reported.

### Timing and feedback

- Timing sections are modeled as three slabs rather than a tile sequence, so the visual mismatch is structural.
- Hit and miss share one removal path, so miss continuation is impossible without splitting presentation behavior by resolved outcome.
- Feedback has alpha/lifetime only; no deterministic transform animation exists.
- No shadow scene-object type or pool exists.

## Alternative Hypotheses

### Markers

1. **Single-material contrast plus ignored apparent-size request — highest likelihood.** Directly supported by source and asset data.
2. **Late transparent compositing — lower likelihood.** Markers are camera-nearer World-opaque objects with depth writes, but a marker-only grid/track/background toggle oracle is still needed.
3. **Missing explicit normals — lower likelihood.** The deterministic GLB has positions/indices but no normal accessor; synthesized/faceted lighting could reduce volume readability, though it does not explain alpha.
4. **Actual alpha transparency — contradicted.** Asset and runtime alpha/depth evidence are opaque.

### Obstacles

1. **Lossy legacy height normalization — confirmed.** Exact real-map and ArcViewer/AeroBeat source comparison differ at `h=3` versus `height=1`.
2. **Wrong horizontal width — contradicted for the 37.04 s wall.** Source `_width` is exactly `1`, and both systems retain one horizontal lane.
3. **Wrong Z duration — not the reported difference.** Source duration is very short (`0.0625` beat), and AeroBeat retains interval endpoints; prior interval-Z tests pass.
4. **Difficulty mismatch — contradicted.** The same nearby type-1 wall pattern exists in all five difficulties.

## Why Previous Fixes Failed

- The arrow correction changed only directional-arrow source geometry and target/grid layering. The marker source and separate cursor path were unchanged.
- Marker automation asserted count, rough pixel presence, and basic occlusion, not physical contrast/coverage against realistic backgrounds.
- Existing obstacle tests verify orientation and supported 4×3 cell emission. They encode the current lossy type-1 mapping rather than compare source continuous geometry with a reference viewer.
- Existing timing tests validate exact interval bounds but not lane-surface alignment or tiled presentation.
- Existing removal tests deliberately require identical `0/40/79/80 ms` hit/miss behavior, so they preserve the now-rejected miss presentation.

## Unknowns

1. Derrick’s desired gameplay semantic for `3c9d` type-1 walls:
   - make all canonical cells in that column visually and collision-blocked,
   - preserve current collision cell but render the source-faithful continuous wall extent, or

2. Relative marker contribution from fixed size, structural contrast, and missing normals.
3. Preferred exact amplitudes for `Great` bounce and `Miss` shake. These can be bounded renderer tuning values and visually reviewed without changing gameplay timing.
4. Tile pitch/count policy for partial timing intervals. Recommended default is deterministic fixed Z pitch with clipped boundary tiles so exact timing extents remain authoritative.

## Proposed Verification

Before implementation:

- Marker pixel oracle across direct/iframe portrait/landscape DPR 1/3, black/white/camera-like fields, and all eight environments: area, alpha invariance, edge/interior luminance contrast, grid/track/timing on/off, exact apparent size, foreground/background depth, and lifecycle teardown.
- Real `3c9d` fixture pinned by version hash and exact obstacle records. Assert source parse, normalized geometry, emitted cells, any new visual-geometry field, renderer world footprint, collision occupancy, and ArcViewer-reference interpretation independently.
- Timing tiles: exact total segment span, fixed pitch, clipped boundary tile, per-presentation lane centers/widths/gaps, track-top Y bias, draw layer, and variable counts for changed timing data.
- Miss: monotonic Z through and beyond zero, gray tint only, opacity/depth retained, absent exactly at bounded expiry; hit retains exact white flash and `80 ms` removal.
- Feedback: deterministic absolute-elapsed keyframes and larger minimum apparent bounds; no timer, score, event, or state mutation.
- Shadows: exact recipient count, source X/Z linkage, floor/track-top projection, obstacle interval footprint, target-over-shadow depth, miss continuation, pool/context/detach cleanup, and no gameplay/public-contract effect.

## Recommended Fix

### Marker

Create one append-only gameplay-asset successor changing only `athlete-marker/sphere-v1`:

- one canonical sphere reused for nose and wrists,
- explicit tintable core plus stable white/dark structural geometry/materials,
- explicit normals if compatible with deterministic tooling,
- preserve bounds initially unless the pixel oracle proves the physical footprint inadequate.

Update renderer appearance logic so role tint affects only the marker core and structural materials remain intact. Honor a bounded apparent-size contract consistently across viewport/DPR/camera or replace the unused `sizeCssPx` request with one explicit tested world/pixel policy. Preserve arrow bytes and confirmed-good arrow behavior.

### Obstacle

Do not patch wall scale blindly. First choose the intended visual-versus-collision semantic with Derrick. The smallest robust architecture is to preserve normalized source obstacle geometry separately from canonical collision cells, then let the renderer reproduce the selected continuous visual extent while gameplay consumes explicit canonical occupancy. If Derrick instead intends a full canonical column to be blocked, update conversion and gameplay fixtures explicitly and audit the changed avoidance semantics.

### Timing tiles

Replace the three broad elevated slabs with deterministic lane-surface tile objects. Keep exact timing-window Z boundaries, derive variable tile count from segment length and fixed pitch, clip boundary tiles, use four canonical columns for Flow/Grid and two lane centers for Boxing Lanes, and render with a tiny anti-z-fighting track-top bias before targets. Do not change judgement windows or the white target flash.

### Miss/Great and shadows

- Hit: retain current confirmed flash and exact `80 ms` removal.
- Miss: retain through the existing bounded resolved window, compute moving Z from authoritative timeline, gray only tintable role materials, and remove at expiry.
- Increase feedback apparent size. Drive `Miss` horizontal shake and `Great` bounce from existing absolute elapsed milliseconds with deterministic bounded keyframes.
- Add renderer-only flattened dark translucent shadow objects directly beneath every visible note/bomb/guard instance and source-faithful obstacle footprint. Depth test on, depth write off, no lighting shadows, and lifecycle tied to the source object.
