# 0.0.68 debug — Saber glow readability

## Exact observed failure

Derrick's 825×226 screenshot (`4205397d43e7b391e0fbbdbb01d5cb063f76dbb4af55c79ec41216d4907b8d27`) shows the now-aligned cyan halo as a bright opaque white/cyan cylinder, not emitted light. A read-only isolated renderer probe on `#02040A`, scale 2, Z=90°, measured one antialias edge pixel followed by a flat clipped plateau: background `2,4,10`; edge `26,67,72`; interior `98,255,255`.

## Expected behavior

A readable opaque core must be surrounded by a wider, softer, lower-energy additive halo with bounded radial/endpoint falloff. The result must remain aligned and color-preserving on dark/bright backgrounds, GLB/fallback, scale 2, and combined XYZ.

## Execution path

Assembly resolves one canonical Flow pose and sends it to renderer. Renderer `stageSaber()` applies the pose to one root, then `acquireSaberGlow()` creates a larger child cylinder. The glow material uses full hand color for diffuse+emissive, gain 1, opacity 1, `BLEND_ADDITIVE` (`ONE,ONE`), no culling, depth test on, depth write off, no map/falloff, and no bloom. Existing browser tests reduce framebuffer deltas to binary masks and assert alignment/containment only.

## Most likely root cause

Confirmed: the halo is a larger uniformly shaded full-gain cylinder with a hard silhouette, not a falloff shader. Its opacity is ignored by `ONE,ONE` blending; double-sided front/back contributions and linear exposure clip cyan channels. Alignment is correct and unrelated.

## Alternative hypotheses

- Tone mapping/exposure contributes to clipping but cannot create spatial falloff.
- Backface accumulation increases energy but culling alone still leaves a hard cylinder.
- Pose/root, GLB core, and bright environment hypotheses are contradicted by isolated dark-background reproduction and overlap 1.0.

## Why previous fixes failed

Prior changes fixed alignment, hilt overlap, and visibility by enlarging/moving/brightening the cylinder. The oracle checked only binary containment, so a larger opaque cylinder passed.

## Unknowns

Final gain/falloff constants and end-on appearance need target-display tuning. Automated tests should bound falloff and clipping rather than encode subjective final art.

## Minimal reproduction

Render baseline, core-only, and halo-only at scale 2 against `#02040A`; sample a perpendicular scanline. Current halo transitions in one AA pixel to a uniform clipped plateau.

## Proposed verification

Add differential dark/bright framebuffer rows for GLB/fallback and cardinal/combined XYZ. Require ordered halo energy `inner > middle > outer > outside`, at least three distinct nonzero bands, low clipped-pixel fraction, preserved role hue, wider support than core, and core containment/readability. Include today's uniform cylinder as a negative control.

## Recommended fix

Keep root/core geometry and collision unchanged. Replace the uniform glow response with a bounded radial+endpoint attenuation shader/material using alpha-aware additive composition (`SRC_ALPHA,ONE`) and one surface contribution. Do not ship only lower gain/opacity, filmic tone mapping, or culling; none alone creates falloff.
