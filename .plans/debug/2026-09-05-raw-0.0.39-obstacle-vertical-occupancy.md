## Exact Observed Failure

Derrick physically compared raw `0.0.39` with BeatSaver ArcViewer for `3c9d`, `Dance Dance Revolution [DDRMIX]`, and reported:

> “the obstacles are appearing as a larger column now, but they should be taking up the other grid cells below them in the test DDR song we're testing against.”

Derrick then clarified the compatibility requirement:

> “we're trying to match the contract beatsaver gives us. The obstacles in the DDR song map appear in the top row, and extend down to the bottom row. Our older contract was single cell only, which was incorrect. Our updated contract was multi cell is allowed, however the obstacles in the same timestamp we're testing against are appearing above the within the top row and above the cell grid, not taking up the middle and bottom rows to match the beatsaver arcviewer app. So our contract is either incorrect or not being enforced correctly.”

The screenshot shows raw `0.0.39` now renders a continuous tall wall, but it is anchored at the top grid row and extends upward outside the grid. It does not occupy the middle and bottom cells that ArcViewer visibly covers at the comparison timestamp.

## Most Likely Root Cause

**The updated contract is enforced consistently, but its vertical normalization/coordinate contract is wrong for the required BeatSaver/ArcViewer-compatible footprint.**

The prior implementation correctly added continuous multi-cell geometry, but encoded the type-1 height using a bottom-origin/upward interpretation all the way into AeroBeat world space. Derrick's external oracle requires the normalized top-row anchor to extend downward through the bounded canonical grid.

Therefore:

- this is not a facade enforcement failure;
- `gridMask:[1]`, renderer center Y `3`, and top-only collision are consistent symptoms of the wrong normalized contract;
- the tests certify the incorrect acceptance rule and must be replaced, not preserved;
- calling `[1,5,9]` “fake” was incorrect relative to the clarified compatibility requirement.

The earlier diagnosis mistakenly promoted the current local `beatsaber_lane_layer` convention over Derrick's direct ArcViewer comparison. It also conflated ArcViewer's intermediate legacy `y=2,h=3` record with the required AeroBeat top-left-grid footprint. That conclusion is superseded by this record.

## Alternative Hypotheses

1. **Contract is correct but renderer ignores it — contradicted.** Renderer uses the exact supplied geometry and produces the mathematically expected upward AABB.
2. **Mask derivation alone is wrong — incomplete.** Changing only `[1]` to `[1,5,9]` would leave visual and collision authority above-grid, creating a dangerous divergence.
3. **GLB pivot, opacity, or layer hides lower geometry — contradicted.** No lower geometry is generated; facade/entity AABB matches model output.
4. **Wrong map/difficulty/timestamp — contradicted by current golden.** All five Standard difficulties contain the same type-1 obstacle at approximately `37.04 s` (ExpertPlus differs only slightly in beat time), and the exact Hard source digest is pinned.

## Why Previous Fixes Failed

- The previous debug pass stopped after reading ArcViewer's intermediate `y=2,h=3` conversion and did not make the physically observed ArcViewer footprint an end-to-end pixel/occupancy oracle.

## Recommended Fix

### Correct the normalized contract

Version the Flow obstacle contract so it no longer labels canonical gameplay geometry as raw bottom-origin `beatsaber_lane_layer` geometry.

Preserve two explicit records:

1. `sourceGeometry`/source metadata: exact provider provenance and legacy conversion evidence.
2. `gameplayGeometry`: normalized AeroBeat top-left-grid render/collision/occupancy authority.

For the exact v2 type-1 event:

- source evidence retains type `1` and ArcViewer intermediate `x1/y2/w1/h3`;
- normalized gameplay geometry becomes `{coordinateSpace:"aerobeat_top_left_grid",x:1,y:0,width:1,height:3}`;
- height extends downward;
- `gridMask` derives only from gameplay geometry and equals `[1,5,9]`.

Do not treat this as a UI-only adaptation. It is the corrected provider-compatibility normalization for AeroBeat's canonical coordinate domain.

### Runtime and persistence

- Authoring converts provider geometry once at the coordinate-space boundary.
- Content and assembly preserve source evidence plus normalized gameplay geometry.
- Renderer and calibrated-nose collision use normalized gameplay geometry only.
- Validation rejects mixed coordinate spaces, mismatched masks, old upward pairs under the new schema, malformed source containers, conflicts, unsupported rotations/types, and stale provenance.
- Existing prior-contract stored packages remain list/export/delete-only and require reimport for Play/Test; migrations label provenance only and never rewrite stored bytes or hashes.

### Required regressions

- Multi-lane width, partial-height, boundary clipping, malformed geometry, and sort/uniqueness masks.
