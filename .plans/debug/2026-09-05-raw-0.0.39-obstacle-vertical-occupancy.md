# Raw 0.0.39 obstacle vertical-occupancy diagnosis

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Physical-review Bead:** `aerobeat-web-assembly-k72.17`  
**Defect Bead:** `aerobeat-web-assembly-mzz`  
**Candidate:** immutable raw `0.0.39`, tree `799c9b346f1e1bffc96bf8e0cd01d8edd5e33928`  
**Disposition:** Physical revision recorded; diagnosis complete; implementation requires approval of the product-semantic change below. Raw `0.0.39` remains immutable and rejected.

## Exact Observed Failure

Derrick physically tested raw `0.0.39` against BeatSaver `3c9d`, `Dance Dance Revolution [DDRMIX]`, and reported:

> “the obstacles are appearing as a larger column now, but they should be taking up the other grid cells below them in the test DDR song we're testing against.”

The supplied screenshot shows continuous tall translucent red wall columns, but the DDR type-1 wall is anchored at the top grid row and extends above the visible 4×3 playfield rather than covering the middle and bottom cells below it.

Directly observed:

- Raw `0.0.39` fixed raw `0.0.38`’s one-square rendering by restoring a continuous `2.94`-high wall.
- The resulting column does not occupy the other displayed cells below its top-row intersection.
- Derrick expects the complete displayed lane column below that top cell to be occupied.

Inferred and then confirmed from source/runtime:

- The exact Hard event is v2 `_type:1`, `_lineIndex:1`, `_width:1`, beat `92.5999984741211`, duration `0.0625` beat.
- Current normalization emits source geometry `{x:1,y:2,width:1,height:3}` and derived bounded `gridMask:[1]`.
- Renderer and collision apply that geometry faithfully; this is not a facade scale, GLB pivot, opacity, layer, or hidden-wall defect.

## Expected Behavior

Derrick’s newest physical expectation supersedes the prior AeroBeat acceptance lock for this product presentation:

- A v2 type-1 DDR obstacle anchored in top-row lane `1` must occupy the displayed cells below it as one continuous full displayed column: `[1,5,9]`.
- The wall must remain one continuous visual, not three gapped cubes.
- Visual, grid occupancy, and calibrated-nose collision authority must agree; no UI-only fake collision cells.
- X lane, width, time interval, source provenance, directly-below shadow, and all unrelated approved visuals remain unchanged.
- Raw `0.0.39` remains byte-immutable; the correction lands only in an append-only successor after coder→QA→audit.

This is a deliberate AeroBeat gameplay adaptation of Beat Saber v2 type-1 crouch/ceiling semantics. It must not be mislabeled as source-identical geometry.

## Execution Path

1. `aerobeat-web-content-authoring/src/beatmap.js::normalizeV2Obstacle()` maps v2 type `1` to `y=2,height=3`.
2. `aerobeat-web-contracts/src/flow-obstacle-contracts.js::deriveFlowObstacleGridMask()` treats `y` as bottom-most source layer, extends height upward, clips to source layers `0..2`, then maps `(2-sourceLayer)*4+column`.
3. For `{x:1,y:2,width:1,height:3}`, only source layer `2` intersects the bounded grid, so the exact mask is `[1]`.
4. Authoring emits the continuous geometry and mask; content and gameplay validate exact agreement.
5. Gameplay collision maps calibrated nose evidence with `sx=4*nx-0.5`, `sy=2.5-3*ny`, then intersects continuous bounds `x-0.5..x+width-0.5` and `y-0.5..y+height-0.5`.
6. Current Y collision bounds are `1.5..4.5`: top-screen/head evidence can contact while middle/bottom evidence avoids.
7. Assembly `session-render-projection.js::flowObstacleTarget()` forwards `cells:[...gridMask]` and unchanged geometry.
8. Renderer `gameplay-scene-model.js` calculates center `(-0.5,3)`, dimensions `0.94×2.94×0.15`, and world Y AABB `1.53..4.47`. Only top-row cell `1` intersects; the rest extends above the grid.
9. Renderer facade applies that transform directly. Grid/timing layers do not hide missing lower geometry.

## Most Likely Root Cause

The previous correction correctly implemented the then-approved Beat Saber v2 type-1 interpretation: source `y=2` is a bottom anchor and height extends upward. Derrick’s physical expectation instead requires cell `1` to act as the top anchor of a downward full displayed column.

Therefore the root cause is a product-semantic mismatch, not a rendering bug:

- `gridMask:[1]` is a correct consequence of the existing geometry contract.
- Center Y `3` and collision Y `1.5..4.5` are also internally correct under that contract.
- Raw `0.0.39` acceptance tests explicitly lock behavior now rejected physically.

The fix must version and truthfully distinguish source geometry from AeroBeat gameplay projection rather than silently changing the meaning of `beatsaber_lane_layer` geometry.

## Alternative Hypotheses

1. **Grid mask derivation bug — contradicted.** Direct calculation and contract tests prove `[1]` for upward `y=2,height=3`; render/collision do not use the mask as their geometry authority.
2. **Renderer Y inversion or pivot defect — contradicted.** Scene-model and facade AABBs match the authored continuous geometry exactly.
3. **Opaque grid/timing overlay hides lower wall — contradicted.** There is no lower wall geometry to hide; layers are translucent and current AABB ends at the top-row lower edge.
4. **Wall asset scale defect — contradicted.** Audited wall source is `0.94×0.94×1`; model scaling intentionally yields `0.94×2.94`.
5. **UI-only expectation — possible but rejected as design.** Painting `[1,5,9]` while collision remains top-only would visually communicate collision where none exists.

## Why Previous Fixes Failed

- Raw `0.0.38` discarded continuous obstacle height and rendered one square. Raw `0.0.39` restored height, but the plan explicitly selected the ceiling/crouch interpretation and forbade `[1,5,9]`.
- Existing tests are not missing the behavior: they deliberately assert `[1]`, center `(-0.5,3)`, height `2.94`, top-only collision, and middle avoidance.
- Asset footprint, opacity, layering, timing-tile, and shadow changes were orthogonal to vertical anchor semantics.

## Recommended Fix

### Contract and data model

Introduce a versioned obstacle record that preserves two explicit truths:

1. `sourceGeometry`: faithful Beat Saber lane/layer provenance.
2. `gameplayGeometry`: AeroBeat render/collision/occupancy authority.

For v2 type `1`:

- `sourceGeometry = {x:1,y:2,width:1,height:3}`;
- `gameplayGeometry = {x:1,y:0,width:1,height:3}`;
- `gridMask` derives only from `gameplayGeometry` and equals `[1,5,9]`.

For v2 type `0`, preserve the existing full-height source/gameplay interpretation. For v3/v4 explicit geometry, default gameplay geometry to source geometry unless a separately documented adaptation is required; do not invert all modern explicit maps.

Do not silently reinterpret version-1 `coordinateSpace:"beatsaber_lane_layer"` records. Existing stored packages remain export/list/delete-only and require reimport for Play/Test under the new contract. Migrate persistence provenance labels only; never rewrite stored bytes or hashes.

### Runtime

- Content and assembly preserve both geometries and validate exact gameplay-mask agreement.
- Flow renderer and calibrated-nose collision use `gameplayGeometry` only.
- Provenance/debug evidence exposes `sourceGeometry` without making it collision authority.
- Continue rendering one continuous wall and one directly-below inert shadow.

### Required regression matrix

- Exact `3c9d` Hard: source geometry unchanged; gameplay geometry `x1/y0/w1/h3`; mask `[1,5,9]`; center `(-.5,1)`; `0.94×2.94×0.15`; all three rows contact.
- V2 type `0`: full-column behavior unchanged.
- V3/V4 explicit `y1/h2`: identity projection and correct top/middle bounded mask; no global inversion.
- Malformed, conflicting, unsupported, legacy, and stale packages fail closed.
- Sparse 15 FPS segment clipping, uncertainty, episode/combo reset, outcome partition, privacy, and lifecycle behavior remain unchanged.
- Direct/real-iframe portrait/landscape DPR matrix proves all three cells are visually covered with Grid on/off.
- Preserve approved arrows, white outlines, right-timing flash, exact 80 ms hit removal, data-derived timing tiles, marker materials/size, gray miss continuation, Great/Miss animations, shadows, and environment order/default.
- Preserve raw `0.0.35–0.0.39` and asset raw/review `0.0.1–0.0.7` exactly.
