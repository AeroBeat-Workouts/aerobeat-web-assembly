# 0.0.72 — Shoulder-based Flow rotation

> **STATUS (2026-09-25): SHIPPED in immutable raw 0.0.72 (served on 5173). Awaiting Derrick's physical playtest to confirm the feel; Bead `aerobeat-web-assembly-z2cx`.**

## Intake (Derrick)

> "The flow gameplay/test mode marker/equipment rotation is based on a center point on the screen. I would like that to change to be based on your shoulders."
>
> Correction: "use the correct shoulder per wrist, not the midpoint of the shoulders."
>
> Fallback: "use the last known position when shoulders are lost, and in test mode keep the center position we use now."

## Design (confirmed with Derrick)

- **Pivot per wrist:** left wrist rotates around the **left shoulder**; right wrist around the **right shoulder** (each saber swings around its own shoulder joint).
- **Fallback:** when a shoulder drops, hold its **last known position**; if never seen, fall back to screen-center.
- **Reset:** the last-known-shoulder cache resets on a new calibration generation (a recalibration remaps the grid).
- **Test mode:** keep the current **screen-center** pivot (no body in Test mode).

## Implementation

- `src/saber-zone-direction.js` — parameterized the radial center (`projectSquareRadialOrientation`, `squareRadialNeutralInfluence`, `squareRadialEdgeWeights`, `squareRadialSaberTarget`) with an optional `center` defaulting to `{x:0.5,y:0.5}`; pre-shoulder callers are byte-identical.
- `src/index.js` `computeFlowQuaternionTargets(graph, poseInput, visualTest)` — resolves the per-hand shoulder anchor, maintains `lastKnownShoulderPivot` (per hand, anchor space) + `lastKnownShoulderCalibrationId`, and passes the y-flipped pivot as the radial center. Test mode passes the screen center.
- `resolveEquipmentPoses` now forwards the `visualTest` flag.

## Oracles

- `scripts/validate-saber-zone-direction.js` — pure center parameterization: neutral at the shoulder, direction→edge targets, influence scaling, backward-compat (default == explicit center), non-finite center rejection.
- `scripts/validate-aero-game-assembly.js` — caller wiring: per-hand shoulder find, last-known fallback, Test-mode center, y-flipped center passed to the field, calibration-generation reset.
- `scripts/validate-equipment-config-live-state.js` — updated the two source oracles to the new 5-arg field call and the `visualTest` forwarding.
