### Debug check: paused interval must not be mistaken for a dropped frame

- **Observed failure and expected behavior:** The focused test measured two consecutive eligible RAFs, stopped Test's frame loop, advanced 60 seconds without RAF, then resumed. Before the fix, the first resumed RAF incorrectly raised the interval count from 2 to 3 (`3 !== 2`). Only continuous eligible RAF intervals should contribute; the paused time must not.
- **Execution path and root cause:** The historical transport calls `stopFrameLoop()` on pause (`src/index.js:669`) and `startFrameLoop()` on resume (`:713`). The diagnostic retained `lastRafAt` across those calls, because no ineligible RAF arrived to clear it. A different session generation would clear it, but ordinary pause/resume does not change generation.
- **Alternatives and failed approaches:** The earlier idle-wall-time split separated setup from active time, but did not reset the adjacent-RAF timestamp. Discarding all long intervals would incorrectly hide genuine first-hit stalls; the test also confirms a continuous-playing ≥233 ms stall remains visible.
- **Corrective action and verification:** The diagnostic now resets `lastRafAt` on existing historical frame-loop stop/start and at new report/reset windows. The same focused test goes RED before, GREEN after; parent independently ran it and rebuilt the pinned candidate. Browser Test transport and real phone performance still require QA/user evidence.
- **Debugging record:** Problem: paused time inflated the display-interval metric. Symptom: 3 intervals instead of 2 after a 60-second pause. Root cause: stale `lastRafAt` across a stopped loop.
