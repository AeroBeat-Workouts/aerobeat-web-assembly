## Intake (Derrick, 2026-09-21 playtest of 0.0.62)

> "Playtested both flow and boxing, I can confirm the models are load in place of the markers."

### I-1 — BUG: gameplay pauses when markers move outside the calibrated area
> "Whenever the markers move outside of the original calibrated area, the game pauses. That should not be the case, as our last update was to make lost tracking *not* pause the gameplay."

### I-2 — Behavior: markers must keep working outside the calibrated grid
> "We should also allow the markers to continue working when they move outside of the grid. Because during large flow swings we end up 'losing tracking' because your markers move outside of the calibrated grid, even though tracking has not been lost."

**W0 grounding (2026-09-21, parent):** cursor AND equipment records are both zeroed by the same gate — `tracking.gameplayPaused === true || safety.freshCalibrationRequired === true` (`gameplay-cursor-records.js:27`, `gameplay-equipment-records.js:62`, `index.js:1044`). Setters live in `aerobeat-web-input/src/body-grid-service.js` + `aerobeat-web-gameplay/src/session-coordinator.js`. One diagnosis covers I-1 + I-2.

### I-3 — Saber glow reads disconnected from the blade (screenshot 1)
**W0 grounding (2026-09-21, parent):** the GLB is geometrically CONTINUOUS — `HILT_Y1 = BLADE_Y0 = 0.18` (`build-flow-saber-v2.py`). The seam is the r2b gain-1.0 additive glow capsule, which starts at **y=0.15 — inside the hilt** (hilt ends 0.18) — rendered as a bright white-wash band over bright Aero that visually severs dark hilt from blue blade. The original r2 spec was "blade section + tip halo only, hilt excluded" — the 0.15 start already violated it. Fix candidates: (a) glow start → `BLADE_Y0` (0.18); (b) gain falloff profile near the hilt end; (c) both. (a) recommended — one constant, restores the original spec. NOTE: any pixel change re-anchors the saber locked-look oracle (signed-off look changes with this fix — Derrick's screenshot is the new baseline).

### I-4 — Feature: Test-mode equipment config (foundation feature)
> "We need an easier way for me to configure these assets. Lets think through how we can expose the markers and the equipped models so they appear in the Test mode, that way we can introduce new options to modify the transform (scale and rotation) of the equipped models to the hands. The flow and boxing would have unique saved configs we can load the defaults in the build, modify, and save the config yaml so I can pass a finalized config to you to use as the new defaults for the lightsaber and gloves."

Design + implement: Test-mode surface showing markers + equipped models; per-mode (flow/boxing) transform config (scale, rotation relative to hand); load defaults from the build; modify + save YAML; pipeline to bake a finalized YAML into build defaults. **Foundation for I-7 and I-8.**

### I-5 — Saber cut aftermath: slice position + half separation
> "The sword cutting animation sort of works today, but when a beat is cut, it doesn't necessarily happen from the spot the sword cut, its always at the same halfway point, and the parts that corpse that appears may not line up with how rotation or seperation should work (the two corpse pieces should fall to the left and right farther, that way they don't rotate into each other and clip the two halves of the corpse.)"

Implement: (a) slice plane at the blade's actual cut position (not the fixed midpoint); (b) wider lateral separation so the two tumbling halves don't clip each other.

### I-6 — Design-first: velocity-based boxing corpse variations
> "The boxing corpse animation isn't bad, I'm curious to see if playtesters like the smack animations or if they are too similar (there's no amount of randomization / velocity thats taken into account). Perhaps we could create a few variations based on the incoming velocity of the boxing gloves and load the proper predefined animation for the corpse path."

Design a small set of predefined corpse-path variations keyed to incoming-velocity buckets; A/B with playtesters. **Design artifact first — implementation waits on the playtest signal.**

### I-7 — Feature: glove rotation states from motion + upcoming beat
> "My mind wants the glove to rotate according to the direction I'm moving in. So if we are moving from down to up, the gloves rotate into an uppercut position. If we are moving towards a straight punch, even though we are technically moving up in camera space, in the reality of the athlete's real-world space, their wrist is moving forward towards the camera, so if the upcoming beat in boxing is a straight punch, then instead of rotating towards the uppercut rotation while moving up, we move into the straight punch rotation values. Then for guards, we rotate the gloves into a guard rotation, and when we detect we are moving to a horizontal punch, we rotate the glove to match that horizontal punch via its velocity. We would need to talk through this a bit, but we would expose these locked rotations to a config and allow us to configure and save it in our Test mode like we do for our other transform values."

Key insight (Derrick): **camera-space up ≠ real-world motion** — select the rotation from (motion) × (upcoming beat type). Rotation states: straight / uppercut / hook (horizontal) / guard. Locked rotations exposed to the I-4 config. **Depends on I-4.**

### I-8 — Feature: saber rotation by grid position (swing feel)
> "I think our sword for flow could do something similar, but its rotations could be based on where we are in the grid, we would rotate the blade to be down, left, right, up depending on how close we are to the edge of the grid. If your near the center of the grid, that could be another rotation as well. That way as you move your wrists it feels like your 'swinging' the sword instead of it feeling like a static attachment to your markers."

Design + implement: grid-zone → blade-orientation map (4 edges + center); interplay with the current direction-based rotation (replace vs blend); locked orientations exposed to the I-4 config. **Depends on I-4.**
