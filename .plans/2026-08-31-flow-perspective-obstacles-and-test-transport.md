## Goal

Repair missing Flow obstacles, replace the occlusion-prone stationary Flow presentation with an experimental 2.5D perspective approach that preserves the exact 4×3 screen-space hit grid, make Flow arrows/outline crisp at supported DPRs, and add a Visual Test Play/Pause + scrub timeline with live forward/backward rendering and a right-side `mm:ss` timecode.

Keep the implementation fundamentally 2D in WebGL2 and preserve a clean normalized depth/travel seam so a later approved plan can replace projection with full 3D if physical testing justifies it.

## User-approved direction

Derrick reported on the same Easy DDR song used for prior Flow testing:

- Flow obstacles do not appear.
- Stationary same-cell cues obscure one another and make future beats hard to read.
- Flow arrows and their white outline are visibly low resolution while the timing ring is crisp.
- Visual Test needs Play/Pause and a bottom seek timeline; scrubbing must animate cues forward and backward, with `00:00`-style timecode at the right.

Derrick approved:

- explicit obstacle repair;
- 2.5D perspective sprites first, reserving full 3D for a later decision;
- higher-resolution icon rendering;
- a Visual Test transport with deterministic live scrubbing.

## Current diagnosis

### Missing obstacles

`session-render-projection.js` currently recognizes notes, guards, squats, and weaves, then treats every other authored type as a punch. The hypothesis is confirmed: the minimal authored Flow fixture `{type:"obstacle",start:2,end:4,cells:[1,2,5,6]}` projects as `{kind:"punch",family:"straight",cell:null,cells:[]}` and therefore produces no target rectangle. Source/runtime contracts retain exact obstacle `start`, `end`, and occupied `cells`; the repair must also preserve its active interval rather than emitting a one-frame hatch.

### Same-cell occlusion

Flow cues currently remain fixed at their destination cell. Time is represented by fade and a contracting procedural ring. Same-cell targets therefore share one rectangle, and current same-layer ordering can allow a later cue to cover a nearer cue. Merely reversing ordering is insufficient; cues need distinct projected positions/scales by approach depth.

### Low-resolution arrows

Canonical SVG masks are rasterized into an atlas with a default 64 px cell and an 8% inset, leaving roughly 54 px of glyph detail. The white outline magnifies the same mask. The timing ring is not DOM; it is procedurally generated by the WebGL fragment shader at the DPR-aware canvas resolution.

### Scrubbing seam

The audio service already owns pause/seek/duration, and the gameplay coordinator already supports explicit paused-clock synchronization. Visual Test is unscored and derives synthetic presentation from current timeline position, making it the correct bounded surface for backward/forward inspection. Scored Play must not gain arbitrary rewind semantics in this plan.

## Product and rendering model

### 2.5D Flow approach

- Each pending Flow cue has a normalized linear approach progress derived only from authoritative event time and approach lead.
- At progress 0, the cue is small and centered at a bounded vanishing point.
- At progress 1, the cue lands exactly on its existing 4×3 cell rectangle.
- Position and physical size interpolate deterministically; no scoring or source placement changes.
- Far cues draw first and near cues draw last, independent of source-array ordering.
- The Flow approach horizon and assembly publication horizon must agree; cues published beyond the motion lead may not pile up at one exact vanishing-point rectangle.
- Same-cell cues at different times therefore occupy distinct positions/scales throughout the visible approach horizon until arrival.
- The procedural approach ring remains centered on the final cell and contracts to the hit instant, retaining the exact 2D timing affordance.
- Flow obstacles use the same approach-depth model and become translucent projected planes over their exact occupied cells; duration/extent must remain truthful.
- Flow feedback remains attached to the final hit cell with the existing exact 350 ms lifetime.
- Boxing Lanes and Boxing Grid presentation remain unchanged.

### Crisp icon path

- Rasterize canonical SVG masks at a bounded DPR-aware size sufficient for the largest supported Flow cue/outline.
- Minimum accepted atlas cell target is 256 px unless measured browser evidence proves a smaller dynamic bound is equivalent.
- Preserve alpha-only semantic recoloring, white outline behavior, cancellation, context restoration, and truthful degradation.
- Validate visible edge quality at portrait/landscape DPR1/3, including enlarged near-hit arrows.

### Visual Test transport

- Visible only while a downloaded package is in Visual Test or paused Visual Test state.
- Bottom safe-area-aware bar: Play/Pause button, native accessible range input, right-side current `mm:ss` timecode.
- Duration comes from authoritative audio status; seek bounds clamp to that duration.
- Beginning a drag freezes Visual Test audio, records whether it was playing, and keeps the visual frame live.
- Input updates are generation-bound and animation-frame-coalesced: audio seeks, paused gameplay timeline synchronizes, and the renderer immediately projects the selected time.
- Backward scrubbing must remove future synthetic feedback/cues and reconstruct only the selected timeline state.
- Releasing remains paused unless an explicit approved interaction rule and test preserve prior playing state; default for this plan is remain paused for inspection.
- Scored Play does not expose the scrubber and retains all judgement/score invariants.
- Host/iframe public privacy remains bounded; no audio bytes, raw events, or service objects cross UI intents.
