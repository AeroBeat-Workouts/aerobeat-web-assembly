## Decisions (Derrick, unless noted)
- **B3/B9 aftermath art:** replace the white-sphere slice with a **cut-in-half arrow .glb**, **grayed out** when hit. (Boxing straight punches from orb "any" notes keep the **orb** shape, grayed.)
- **B1/B2 collider:** stay glued to the note's white-cube center the whole time the beat is **active**; **deactivate (hide) once the beat resolves** (hit or miss).
- **B9 tuning controls:** expose the punch-placement controls is a **follow-up to discuss** ("we can discuss this to expose the right controls next update") — file as a 0.0.56 follow-up Bead, do NOT block the cycle on it.
- Keep the "punches are fun" behavior intact.
