## Goal and interpretation

Establish how fast the **same production camera → CPU-WASM MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite float16 Worker → native AeroBeat input/markers** can run on Derrick's phone without PlayCanvas, authored chart, environment, beat/obstacle projection, gameplay coordinator, score, hit/corpse effects, audio or 3D equipment meshes. Show only the mirrored live camera and small local left/right wrist and nose marker dots, calibration/recording status and the private Download JSON button. The markers use the real Worker output and native body-grid calibration/validity; screen coordinates come from measured camera landmarks transformed through the mirrored, `object-fit:cover` video crop, not from body-grid-normalized game coordinates. No pixels/landmarks/coordinates leave the page or enter the report. Never label it a real Play run or compare marker presentation opportunities with Play scene draws as if they are equivalent.

Use `defaultLiveCameraConstraints()`, the exact `createMediaPipeWorkerPoseAdapter` pinned model/WASM assets/profile, `createLockedProductionCvService` with 15/s ceiling and **unmodified `createLockedVideoFrameSource`**, the same sourceId/mirror/VideoFrame worker path and real native `createAeroBodyGridService`. Do **not** copy the old isolated benchmark's `uniqueFrameSource()` freshness gate: production Play admits a frame from a ready video element on every available capped CV tick and may reuse an image. Keep camera, Worker, native input, marker positioning and recorder active; do not hide the video/stop the display or fabricate pose output to make numbers green.

A user tap starts a 3-second warmup after real camera/CV/calibration readiness, then one 60-second window. `?preview=1` gives only a short invalid-as-phone-evidence QA run. Every report is an explicit bounded scalar allowlist with a **different schema and purpose** (`camera_cv_marker_baseline`, not `play`) and build/manifest identity. Comparable fields: video-element distinct media-time callbacks (not guaranteed unique sensor captures); CV submitted/poses/in-flight-busy drops; native measured input and valid/held marker counts; Worker runtime inference and adapter round-trip p50/p95; display-weighted age from CV admission; camera resolution/configured frame rate. Baseline-specific UI rAF count must be labeled `markerPresentationFrames`, never `sceneFrames`. No raw frames, screenshots, landmarks, coordinates, chart/media/device IDs, per-frame timing arrays, network upload, storage, public snapshots/events, or iframe messages. Reject hidden tab, camera/Worker restart or loss, geometry/rotation change, calibration reset, no frame/no pose, source/counter reset; stop camera and Worker on unload. Use a clean source-pinned build and SHA-256 manifest of **every** emitted HTML/JS/Worker/WASM/model asset; dev without sidecar and QA preview always invalid as physical data.
