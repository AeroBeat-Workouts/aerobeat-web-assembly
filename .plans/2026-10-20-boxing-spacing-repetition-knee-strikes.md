## Section B — Reduce repetitive Boxing patterns

- **B1: family-preserving hand alternation** (`gmnl.4`; authoring + Assembly). Add `Repeated punch: switch hand (%)` in Boxing Game Setup/pause, integer 0–100, default 0 and reimport-required. For *consecutive same-hand punches of the same family*, deterministically seeded decisions may flip the later punch to the opposite hand while retaining its straight/hook/uppercut family. Recompute lane/target/direction, event IDs, lineage, optimizer eligibility and traces; do not merely relabel a left-hand collision as right-hand. No post-import randomness, no changes to Flow. Treat existing source guards separately.
- **B2: repeated guards** (same Bead). When a run of guards is encountered, a **separate guard replacement percentage** may replace a guard with one seeded straight, hook, or uppercut, assigning a feasible hand/lowest-risk target rather than spawning multiple simultaneous punches. Use the approved independent `Repeated guards: replace with punch (%)` control, integer 0–100, default 0. Do not add status wording. Respect Section A spacing, obstacles, timing windows, score semantics and source provenance. Keep 0% behavior unchanged.
- **B acceptance:** fixture charts prove 0% baseline, 100% rule applicability, intermediate seeded repeatability and retained valid hit targets for all three punch families; guard replacement never violates spacing or obstacle reach. Reimport one selected song, visually inspect authoring trace and test real Boxer Play/Test scoring without multiplying hit events. Derrick playtests strain and repetition before any later lock-in.
