Observed: 2,636 scene draws **43.933/s**, 1,637 distinct video-element `mediaTime` presentations **27.283/s** (configured camera 640×480 at 30/s, not guaranteed unique sensor captures), 413 CV submissions, 413 pose outputs and 413 fresh pose consumptions **6.883/s**, 474 in-flight busy drops, 320 no-fresh-pose input advances, 390 fresh processed two-equipment pose pairs **6.5/s**, all 390 yielded two poses, 26 were marked held. Worker `detectForVideo` inference p50 **107.1ms**, p95 **135.3ms**; adapter Worker round-trip p50 **117.9ms**, p95 **150.4ms**. Display-weighted CV-admission pose age p50 **176.2ms**, p95 **253.4ms**; scene-interval p50 20.4ms, p95 45.8ms. Pose-age/scene-interval distributions retain the **latest 512 samples**, not the whole 60-second window. Expected for smoother tracking: enough new measured poses and valid equipment updates to keep pace with the intended CV cadence (maximum 15/s); 43.933 scene draws/s must not be misread as pose throughput. The earlier visual estimate of <10 tracking updates/s is consistent with this measurement.

### Execution path and leading cause

`createLockedVideoFrameSource()` samples the live HTML video element (`src/production-cv-service.js:123-133`); video-element callback `mediaTime` uniqueness is counted by `src/phone-play-cv-window.js:60-70`. Production CV schedules an estimate every `ceil(1000/15)` ≈ 67ms and permits **one** in-flight adapter request (`src/production-cv-service.js:49,92-112`). It increments `droppedFrameCount` before admission if the previous estimate still runs. The real Worker times `poseLandmarker.detectForVideo` (`aerobeat-web-vendor-mediapipe/src/mediapipe-worker.js:71-88`), then its adapter times the Worker round trip (`.../mediapipe-worker-adapter.js:315-324`). Completed poses are consumed by the Play display loop (`src/index.js:1830-1884`) and can produce valid equipment poses; scene draws may continue between fresh poses. The reporter reads exact deltas and private distributions (`src/phone-play-cv-window.js:72-100`, `src/private-performance-recorder.js:31-60`).

**Confirmed immediate throughput bottleneck:** the phone's *measured Worker model invocation* takes 107.1ms median—longer than the ~67ms admission interval—and requests remain in flight for 117.9ms median including Worker round trip. Of 887 scheduling attempts (413 admitted + 474 busy), **53.4%** were rejected as already busy. Every admitted request produced an output (413/413) and every output reached fresh Play input (413/413), so there is no evidence of another downstream pose-discard stage dominating this window. That caps practical tracking at the observed 6.883/s despite 27.283/s video-element presentations and 43.933/s scene draws. It **does not yet prove** whether intrinsic model cost, concurrent scene load, browser scheduling/CPU contention or phone thermals cause the 107ms inference time.
