Derrick asked to make the next phone test export JSON from the established private performance-testing pipeline. **Status:** Derrick authorized this next test; implementation in progress. ## Decision and acceptance boundary

Use a separate private diagnostic page that embeds the **real production `<aero-game>` and camera-backed Play session** with an imported chart. Reuse the full-game Test recorder's opt-in on-phone control, source-pinned build, served-asset checks, and touch-triggered JSON download, and the existing phone-performance page's scalar camera/worker timing patterns. Do **not** convert the old CV-idle Test/aftereffect recorder into a purported Play test; keep its 8449 route and the current 8453 private candidate unchanged. Propose a fresh loopback4193/private HTTPS8454 origin only after verifying both are available. A new origin requires chart reimport and camera authorization. Never start Funnel or publish a numbered release.

The on-screen FPS counter samples an independent animation-frame loop (`src/index.js:1759-1799`), **not** actual scene, camera, or pose FPS. The actual display loop consumes timestamp-new CV poses and may redraw held equipment (`src/index.js:1819-1893`). Production CV is a CPU-WASM Worker with a 15-FPS submission ceiling and one in-flight frame (`src/production-cv-service.js:11-15,49,92-114`); none of those facts identifies the phone bottleneck. Existing private scalar recorder (`src/private-performance-recorder.js:3-64`) already captures scene intervals/rate, renderer CPU, worker inference/end-to-end, pose age, submission/pose counts, and sanitized camera settings. Configured camera FPS is **not** measured camera delivery. Production currently stamps a pose at admission, so pose age is **not** sensor-capture-to-screen latency.
