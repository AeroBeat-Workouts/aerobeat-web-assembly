## Derrick's decision and scope

Depth controls already work; keep their meaning (forward 1 is the visible tip, rear 0 the hilt), but remove the arbitrary numeric upper bounds and forward minimum. Collider Scale now resizes the **Flow saber's scored hit volume and Show outline**, not note targets. At 1 it follows the currently visible solid saber; higher values make hits more forgiving, zero has no Flow saber contact. Derrick chose to remove numeric caps from **all three Flow controls**; permit finite nonnegative values, including his examples, not NaN/Infinity or negative lengths. A finite value whose derived geometry cannot be represented without infinity must fail truthfully rather than silently clamp or corrupt scoring. Boxing's existing scale, depth and target semantics remain unchanged. Changing this meaning supersedes Derrick's earlier request to keep Collider Scale separate from saber appearance; visually only the debug hit outline scales, never the authored saber art itself.

## Diagnostic report — before code edits

### Exact observed failure

Derrick says Depth+/- work but Collider Scale is wrong; `1` is not the size of visible equipment and the settings reject `0`, `.00001` and `9999999`. Source confirms: `assembly/src/game-setup-coordinator.js:12,74` enforces Scale `.25–4`, Flow forward `1–4`, rear `0–4`; `assembly/src/index.js:3240` duplicates `.25–4` Scale and `1–4` both depths in native number inputs; `gameplay/src/flow-collider-collision.js:14–21,60–63` enforces those bounded values. This is an observed contract mismatch with Derrick's new requirement, not evidence that the earlier depth fix failed. Actual physical Play rotation is **not yet observed** by Derrick.

### Expected behavior

Flow Scale 1 should yield a hit volume congruent in size with the visible solid saber, not the formerly broader radius `.18 × pose.scale`. Pinning the actual signed saber GLB's accessor bounds shows the emissive blade/tip radius `.024`, hilt radius `.03`, blade tip at `.75` and a separate visual-only halo around the blade radius `.045`. Use the solid blade (not a glow effect) as the Scale1 size authority, and explicitly acknowledge the hilt is wider; do not silently claim a constant-radius capsule exactly copies every artistic contour. Keep reach controlled by Depth independently. Scale0 means no scored Flow saber contact and no visible debug volume. Depth0+rear0 produces a point/sphere or absent zero-radius volume without division-by-zero. All three controls accept user-supplied finite nonnegative values without arbitrary caps. Boxing must not inherit Flow's relaxed UI/settings bounds.

### Execution path

### Most likely root cause

The setting is wired to the note target rather than the saber, and three layers enforce historical range limits. The current scored/overlay saber radius `.18` is 7.5 times the GLB's solid blade radius `.024`; setting Scale1 cannot bridge them. The previously released 0.0.130 correctly draws its scored broad capsule but honors the **prior**, now superseded, instruction that target Collider Scale remain separate from the visual saber; this was not a missed test for the old contract. The new behavior requires changing the shared Flow scoring/visual contract deliberately.

### Alternative hypotheses

1. The volume looks too large solely because of perspective: contradicted by GLB accessor radius `.024` versus scorer radius `.18` before projection.
2. Scale already resizes the saber but overlay fails to update: contradicted by `resolveTargetColliderBounds` multiplying the target half-sizes while both `resolveFlowSaberReach` and scene model use the fixed capsule radius.
3. A generic input-only min/max removal suffices: contradicted by assembly normalization, gameplay settings and contracts; even accepted values would still scale notes.
4. Glow radius `.045` is the intended equipment size: plausible perceptually, but it is a fading material effect, not solid GLB geometry; a screenshot comparison should make any residual interpretation visible for Derrick after the solid-blade correction.
