2. **Fixed-facing score cylinders**: Hold the glove scoring axis in world/track Z and keep wrist-following center, independently of glove art quaternion. Flow saber geometry and wearable art rotations stay unchanged. Diagnosis: `.plans/debug/2026-10-25-boxing-collider-orientation.md`.
3. **Conditional simultaneous knee calibration**: Whenever Boxing Knee strikes is toggled on, require both **measured** knees visible with the current >=0.5 confidence criterion at the same instant as the seven existing upper-body landmarks and full T-pose geometry, for the existing continuous 2000ms hold. Do not require hips/ankles for this visibility gate, change the stricter hip+knee+ankle scoring rule, loosen 150ms evidence freshness, or use predicted/frozen/stale knee landmarks. Left/right knee visibility indicators track current measured confidence live; failed required visibility resets hold. Flow and knee-disabled Boxing retain their current seven-landmark gate and loss/recovery semantics. When knee-toggle/mode eligibility changes during a hold, restart the hold. Retain valid previously committed calibration unless the current session explicitly enters a new calibration flow; record any material alternative before changing it.
